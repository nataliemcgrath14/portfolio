
# Midwest Airbnb Analysis

This project takes another look at the Airbnb listings across Chicago, Columbus, and the Twin Cities using R and dplyr.

## Five Key Findings

1. Hosts with 10 or more listings control 37.14% of Columbus's listings, compared with 17.15% in the Twin Cities.

2. Chicago's top 10% of listings generate 40.73% of its estimated revenue, demonstrating revenue concentration.

3. Entire homes in the Twin Cities generate a median estimated annual revenue of $19,619, providing a benchmark for hosts.

4. Chicago Superhosts have a median price premium of $43.75, while Twin Cities Superhosts have a premium of -$1.38.

5. Columbus has 19.03% of its entire homes classified as mostly taken in October 2026, meaning fewer than half their nights are available.
